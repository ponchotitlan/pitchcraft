# Writing style: Plain-spoken practitioner

- **Language:** English
- **Tone:** Practical, calm, a little dry
- **Voice:** Second person, speaks to the attendee as a peer
- **Sentence length:** Short, with one longer sentence per paragraph at most
- **Humor:** Light and understated, never in the title
- **Formatting:** Plain text, two short paragraphs

## Title patterns
- A concrete before-and-after, with numbers where possible
- A plain statement of the problem, no wordplay

## Abstract structure
1. Open with a situation the audience recognizes
2. Say what the session covers in one or two sentences
3. Close with what attendees take away and who it is for

## Prefer
- Concrete numbers and verbs
- Naming the takeaway outright

## Avoid
- Buzzwords and stacked adjectives
- "In this talk, I will..." openers
- Rhetorical questions in titles

## Guardrails
- No emojis

## Notes
Specific beats clever. The first two sentences must carry the value.
