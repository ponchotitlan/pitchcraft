# Running AI agents your team can [[actually trust]]
Own It, Run It, Trust It

## Agents are already in production
They act on real systems, with real credentials, at 3 a.m.
> The question is no longer *if*, but **who is accountable**.

## [pillars] Three things to get right

## [cards] What every agent needs
- 🪪 **An identity** - Who is acting, and on whose behalf?
- 🔧 **The right tools** - Access that matches the job, nothing more.
- 📚 **The right knowledge** - Runbooks and history it can actually use.

## Rolling it out
1. **Start read-only** - Observe before you let it act.
2. **Add guardrails** - Approvals for anything destructive.
3. **Measure** - Track what it did and what it saved.

## Where most teams start
### Bolt-on bots
- Shared admin token
- No audit trail
### Owned agents
- Own identity, scoped access
- Every action logged
> Pick the right column on day one.
